Bütçem — Privacy policy

2026-10-13-v8

Where is your data stored?

You enter or restore income, expenses, transfers, accounts, categories, goals, payment plans and optional profile details. Business mode also stores customer/supplier names, optional phone numbers and notes, projects, receivables/payables, due dates and payment links locally. Only the name and phone of the contact you select are copied into the form; the entire address book is not read. You can review before saving. These records stay in an encrypted on-device database and are not sent to the developer, Google Play or RevenueCat. Your profile does not need your real name.

Adding entries from receipts

At your request, you can capture or select one receipt using the Google Play services scanner. Google ML Kit text recognition runs on-device; the app does not send the receipt image or recognized text to the developer or a cloud parsing service. Google ML Kit may also send device/app information, installation identifiers, usage/performance metrics, API settings and error codes to Google for diagnostics and usage analytics; receipt images and text are not part of those technical metrics. Scanner components may download on first use; Google Play services are subject to their own terms. The app does not attach the receipt photo to permanent records or backups; scanner temporary files are managed by that service. Only the amount, date, category and note you check and add are saved as a local transaction. Recognition can be incorrect; review the income/expense type and details before confirming.

Analysis and plans

Analysis uses your records. Period net is income minus expenses; account balance also includes opening balance and transfers. Plans are not actual transactions; matching needs your confirmation. Estimates and scenarios are not guarantees or financial advice. Similar records are not automatically deleted.

Premium purchases and connections

Premium is a one-time Google Play purchase. RevenueCat verifies, restores and updates purchase access. On app startup and access checks, a randomly generated app user identifier, purchase tokens/transaction details, product/access status and app, device and connection technical information may be processed by these services. No app account is required; the identifier is not your profile name. Google Play handles card details; the app and developer do not receive your card number.

Backup, export and security

Encrypted backup is optional. Automatic backup updates your selected file while the app runs; there is no scheduled task while closed. Its password uses secure device storage; PIN, biometric templates and database keys are excluded. CSV/JSON exports are readable files. You initiate sharing or moving files to another cloud folder. The operating system verifies biometrics. The app does not request contacts, location, camera or microphone access. Security and recovery cannot be guaranteed in every situation.

Retention and deletion

You can edit, export and reset local records in Settings → Delete local data. Reset removes automatic backup settings and its stored password; lock settings are managed separately. External files and Google Play/RevenueCat purchase records are not deleted. Purchase records are retained by the services to verify access and meet legal obligations; contact support for requests about these records. Uninstalling does not cancel Premium.

Support and international services

If you send support email, your address, message and selected attachments are processed to answer your request. The email button only opens a composer; no files or budget records are sent automatically. Do not send unnecessary identity/financial records, PINs or backup passwords. Gmail, Google Play and RevenueCat may use international infrastructure; RevenueCat uses US hosting. Your local budget records are not transferred to these services. The app has no ads, bank connection, AI assistant or cloud synchronization.

Support record retention and deletion

Necessary correspondence is kept while a request is open. Messages and attachments are deleted within 90 days of closure; due records are checked weekly. Only necessary records are kept separately with a reason for legal retention or disputes. Deletion is documented in a minimal operation log without contents or attachments. The publisher carries out this process; it is not an automatic email deletion guarantee.

Contact for personal data requests

Send support and personal data requests to memira.dev@gmail.com with the subject “Bütçem — Kişisel veri talebi”. Describe your request and relevant correspondence/order date; do not send PINs, passwords, card details or unnecessary identity documents. Statutory request methods and necessary proportionate identity checks remain applicable. Valid requests are answered as soon as possible and within 30 days. The app only opens a draft; it does not automatically send or confirm receipt.
