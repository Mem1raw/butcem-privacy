Using Bütçem

Bütçem provides personal budgeting, business record tracking, accounts, goals and record-based analysis. It does not access bank accounts, make payments or provide professional accounting, tax or investment advice. Results depend on accurate and current entries. Estimates and scenarios are not guaranteed outcomes.

Free use and Premium

Basic income/expense records, transfers, account/category management, goals, basic charts and local backup are free. Premium unlocks advanced comparisons, spending radar, scenarios, goal analysis and budget studio tools. It is a one-time, non-consumable purchase, not a subscription. Google Play shows the regional price and taxes before payment. There is no automatic renewal or monthly fee.

Purchase access and restoration

Lifetime access has no scheduled expiry. Access can be verified with the same Google Play account; use Restore purchases on the Premium page if needed. Budget records are not stored in the purchase account; restoring them requires your backup. If a refund revokes purchase access, Premium closes without deleting local records. Verification needs internet; offline status changes are learned upon connection. Refund requests follow Google Play processes and rules; statutory consumer rights remain.

Device, backup and responsibility

No app account is required. PIN and backup password are separate; the developer cannot recover a lost backup password. Keep a current encrypted copy safely outside your device. Deleting local data does not delete external backups. Use the app lawfully for personal and business records. These terms do not exclude non-waivable liability or consumer/data rights. Material service or data changes are explained in the relevant feature.
