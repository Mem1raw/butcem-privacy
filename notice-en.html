Bütçem — Personal data information

2026-10-13-v8

Processed information and purposes

Budget and profile records are processed on your device for personal budgeting and do not reach the publisher. Support correspondence is processed to answer requests; purchase and technical connection information is used to verify/restore Premium, secure the service and resolve order disputes. No advertising profile is created.

Collection methods and recipients

Local records come from your entries and backup restoration. Support information is collected through emails you send; purchase information is collected electronically through Google Play and RevenueCat connections. Gmail handles support; Google Play and RevenueCat handle purchase access. These services may process data abroad. Only legally required information is disclosed to competent authorities.

Legal grounds

Support and purchases directly related to your request are assessed under Article 5(2)(c) of Turkish Law 6698 where necessary to establish/perform a contract; legally required records under 5(2)(ç); records necessary to establish, exercise or defend rights under 5(2)(e). Necessity and data minimization apply to each operation. International transfers separately require the applicable conditions in Article 9. Reading this text or using the app is not explicit consent.

Your rights

Article 11 of Turkish Law 6698 provides rights to learn whether data is processed, request information about processing/purposes/recipients, seek correction and deletion/destruction where conditions apply, request recipient notification, object to adverse solely automated analysis and seek compensation for unlawful processing. Manage on-device records in the app. Contact the publisher below about support and purchase records.

Scanner technical information

Receipt images and text are processed on-device. Google ML Kit may send device/app information, installation identifiers, API settings, error codes and usage/performance metrics to Google for diagnostics and usage analytics. Receipt images and text are not sent. Google Play services may download components; technical processing is subject to Google’s SDK and privacy terms. International processing must also be considered for these technical details.

Support record retention and deletion

Necessary correspondence is kept while a request is open. Messages and attachments are deleted within 90 days of closure; due records are checked weekly. Only necessary records are kept separately with a reason for legal retention or disputes. Deletion is documented in a minimal operation log without contents or attachments. The publisher carries out this process; it is not an automatic email deletion guarantee.

Contact for personal data requests

Send support and personal data requests to memira.dev@gmail.com with the subject “Bütçem — Kişisel veri talebi”. Describe your request and relevant correspondence/order date; do not send PINs, passwords, card details or unnecessary identity documents. Statutory request methods and necessary proportionate identity checks remain applicable. Valid requests are answered as soon as possible and within 30 days. The app only opens a draft; it does not automatically send or confirm receipt.
